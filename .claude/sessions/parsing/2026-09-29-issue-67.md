# Session: Issue #67 Implied rights (CLOSED)

## Problem

Legal expands Obligation/Liberty into DRRP by holder and `position: active` (legal #141). In EPA 1990 s.20(7), "each enforcing authority shall make registers available for inspection by the public", the public holds an implied Liberty, which is a Right. But fractalaw recorded the public as beneficiary/counterparty, so legal got no Right. There are 233 access-worded provisions in 99 hub laws.
