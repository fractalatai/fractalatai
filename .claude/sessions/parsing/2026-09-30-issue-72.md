# Session: Issue #72 Hohfeld correlatives (PENDING)

**Priority 2** (Jason, 2026-10-01): next after #73's verdict split; its legal migration should ship in the same legal release.

## Problem

Users ask "what are my rights?" and "what can be imposed on me?". Layer 1 records only what an actor itself holds, so claim-rights (duties owed to me), liabilities (exposure to a power) and protections are invisible. The spec's layer 1b (`DRRP-CLASSIFICATION.md`) was agreed with legal on 2026-09-30 (`c9c29fd`):
- counterparty → claim_right / liability / no_right;
- beneficiary of an Obligation → protected;
- `correlatives: [{type, to}]`;
- law-level claim/liability/protected_holder;
- never feeds DRRP or the verdict.
