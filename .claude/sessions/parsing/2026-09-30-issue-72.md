# Session: Issue #72 Hohfeld correlatives (ACTIVE)

**Priority 2** (Jason, 2026-10-01), after #73 (closed). Legal's release with the correlative columns is already live.

## Problem

Users ask "what are my rights?" and "what can be imposed on me?". Layer 1 records only what an actor itself holds, so claim-rights (duties owed to me), liabilities (exposure to a power) and protections are invisible. The spec's layer 1b (`DRRP-CLASSIFICATION.md`) was agreed with legal on 2026-09-30 (`c9c29fd`):
- counterparty → claim_right / liability / no_right;
- beneficiary of an Obligation → protected;
- `correlatives: [{type, to}]`;
- law-level claim/liability/protected_holder;
- never feeds DRRP or the verdict.

## Consistency notes (2026-10-01)

- **HSWA:** s.2(1) Employee = counterparty but s.3(1) Person = beneficiary (both SLM), the same kind of duty. s.3(2) has Employee = beneficiary and Responsible Undertaking = counterparty (noise).
- **On Obligation provisions:** 11,660 counterparty (SLM 8,525) vs 2,616 beneficiary (SLM 1,974).
- **Wording:**
  - Protective wording ("ensure … health/safety/welfare", "protect … from", "not exposed to risks") is on 177 counterparties and 244 beneficiaries, split roughly evenly: the inconsistency.
  - Recipient wording ("notify/send/give/supply/provide/consult …") is on 4,908 counterparties (right) and 683 beneficiaries (probably should be counterparty).
- **Noise examples:** HSE as counterparty to a landlord's gas-safety duty; the Commission as counterparty to a Member State's transposition deadline.
