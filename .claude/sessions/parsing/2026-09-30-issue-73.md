# Session: Issue #73 DRRP over time — as made vs as amended (CLOSED)

## Problem

A user asking "what are my obligations?" shouldn't have to filter out repealed or revoked laws first. Legal's `is_making` must still say what a law imposed while it was live. A single classification per provision can't answer both questions:
- a law revoked in one go should show nothing now;
- a law repealed piecemeal should show its current mixed state;
- an in-force law with repealed sections should claim only its live provisions.

Dotted (stripped) text currently risks being read as evidence of `no_obligations`. The hub has 3,779 dotted substantive provisions across 184 laws; legal has 7,660 rows across 474 laws.
